Odin — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Odin is a personal desktop application. It runs entirely on the computer it is installed on. There is no Odin server, no account, and no analytics.

What it accesses

With your authorisation, Odin reads from services you connect: Jira issues assigned to or filed by you, GitHub pull requests you authored or were asked to review, Slack messages you reacted to, Notion pages you explicitly select, and Gmail messages in your inbox that are unread or starred — sender, subject, date and Gmail's own preview snippet. Access is read-only: Odin never sends, replies, labels, archives or deletes.

Google user data

Odin's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is shown in the app on your own machine and nowhere else: it is never transferred to anyone, never used for advertising, and never read by a human.

Where data goes

Nowhere except your own machine. Requests go directly from the app to the service you connected. Access tokens are stored in ~/.config/odin.json, readable only by your user account, and a local database caches the items shown in the app.

What is never collected

No telemetry, no usage tracking, no crash reporting to the author, and no transmission of your data to any third party. Nobody but you can see what Odin reads, because it never leaves your computer.

Revoking access

Disconnect a service inside Odin to delete its stored token, and revoke the grant in that service's own settings. Deleting ~/.config/odin.json removes every stored credential.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the repository, or contact the author directly.
